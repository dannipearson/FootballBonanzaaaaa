📋 How It Works

⚽ 1. Pick 8 Teams (2 from each pot)

Pick 8 teams from the tournament — exactly 2 from each of the 4 pots. Pots are based on FIFA ranking, seeding and tournament odds.

Your teams earn points for every match they play:

  • Win (90 mins): 3 pts
  • Draw: 1 pt
  • Knockout win (ET/pens): 2 pts
  • Knockout loss (ET/pens): 1 pt
🎰 2. Team Potluck (1 team per pot, per question)

For each question, pick one team from each pot. 5 points for each correct answer.

  • Which team scores the most group stage goals?
  • Which team gets the most cards (yellow + red) in the whole tournament?
  • Which team goes furthest in the tournament?
  • Which team does the worst (wooden spoon of their pot)?

Ties broken by goal difference, goals for/against, or points awarded to both.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 3. Pick 7 Outfield Players

Pick any 7 outfield players (no goalkeepers) from the tournament.

  • Goal: 2 pts
  • Assist: 1 pt
  • Highest Rated Player (WhoScored.com): 2 pts

⭐ Double Unique: if you're the only person to pick a player, all their points are doubled. Some big names have ended up unique before — choose carefully!

💎 3a. Triple Unique Player Bonus

Pick one additional outfield player. If they are completely unique across all pick sections, you earn triple points for everything they do.

If someone else picks them in any slot, you get zero points from this section — so be bold!

Same scoring as section 3 (goals, assists, WhoScored rating).

😈 4. Naughty Step (5 players)

Pick 5 players you think will get carded during the tournament.

  • Yellow card: 2 pts
  • Red card: 5 pts
  • All 5 players carded (yellow or red): +5 bonus pts
  • All 5 players get red cards: +10 bonus pts
🏅 5. The Quarter Finalists (8 teams)

Predict the 8 teams to reach the Quarter Finals.

  • Correct QF team: 2 pts
  • Correct AND unique pick: 4 pts
❓ 6. Competition Awards & Questions

5 points each for correctly predicting:

  • Outright winner of the tournament
  • Wooden Spoon (lowest points + worst GD)
  • Golden Boot (top goalscorer)
  • Golden Ball (player of the tournament)
  • Golden Glove (best goalkeeper)
  • Highest scoring team
  • Total red cards in the tournament
  • Total own goals in the tournament
  • How far will England get?
  • How far will Scotland get?
🔄 7. Last 16 Player Transfer (×2)

After the Round of 32, you can transfer in 2 new outfield players. You keep all points earned so far, and your new players earn points from the Round of 16 onwards.

  • Double Unique still applies — separate pool from your original picks
  • You can pick players already chosen by others in section 3
  • Existing unique picks remain unchanged

You'll be reminded by email/message once the Last 32 concludes. Transfers must be locked in before the first Last 16 game. If you don't submit, picks will be allocated at the organiser's discretion.
